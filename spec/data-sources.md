R4yGM archive, RocketTraker and generic flight simulators remain unselected; this task did not re-certify their code or licenses.

## Deterministic association and profile selection
1. Import LL2 immutable launch UUID, mission ID, operator, pad and vehicle variant. Internal attempt increments on an explicit scrub followed by a new attempt; mere schedule slip increments schedule revision.
2. Route registry entry must hold exact source identity, publication/fetch/hash, approved parser version, mission aliases with evidence, pad and validity interval. Unique mission + pad + operator + overlapping attempt validity is necessary. Multiple matches, filename-only identity or unsupported dogleg -> ambiguous and rejected for reconstruction. Never infer heading from orbit, Starlink name, RTLS landing point or old map.
3. Distinguish published path, hazard corridor, official visibility region and observed sighting. Hazard polygon can produce coarse screening only. Reconstructed candidate routes require independent source-backed path/direction constraints AND a justified simple-route family; direction alone is insufficient if the flown route is structurally ambiguous. Registry records explicit bounds/assumptions and why absent dogleg is supported.
4. Filter profiles by variant, orbit/mission class, pad latitude compatibility, recovery, stage and burn coverage, time origin/datum/downrange. Require documented equivalence for every mismatch; unknown !=compatible. Retain joint path/altitude/time correlations; no arbitrary cross-product of incompatible stage profiles. Never stretch a profile to obtain a positive result. Store association manifest and input hashes.
5. Hold out a different historical mission from template creation. Compare profile shape independently of observer sightings; neither agreement nor synthetic geometry validates human visibility. Promote a registry format only with real raw samples and reject cases tested.

## Parser/retrieval contract
Allowlisted HTTPS sources; bounded redirects/size/time, no private-address SSRF, conditional ETag/Last-Modified where supplied. Cache immutable bytes and parsed results separately. 404 document -> missing; 403 -> inaccessible; 429 -> Retry-After; transient 5xx/timeouts -> exponential backoff. Unknown MIME/version, OCR-only map, missing units, ambiguous midnight rollover, coordinate datum or mission -> unsupported, retain reason and source link; no LLM-generated coordinates are evidence. Changed unparseable source invalidates prior current association; do not silently keep it approved. Retrievable source with unknown publication time remains explicitly unknown and cannot justify fresh positive reminders without an independently verified validity statement.
